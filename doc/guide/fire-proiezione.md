## FIRE › Proiezione — a verdict over tiles

- **The question**: «quanto può valere il portafoglio tra N anni, e con che probabilità?». Accumulation only, no withdrawals (those are the Monte Carlo's). Sixth tab of the page, after «Dopo il FIRE» (the Monte Carlo tab, renamed by T5, 2026-10-05) (`?tab=proiezione` is the name; like the other tabs the page keeps the active tab in state and does not read the URL).
- **No new engine**: `runAccumulationSimulation` with `withdrawalRate: 0` (no path ever reaches FIRE, so every year saves). The three optional parameters are neutral for the Calcolatore: `savingsYears` (the saving of year t enters only while `t ≤ savingsYears`), `collectPaths: false` (no path objects: one `Float64Array` per year, sorted; the percentiles are read from them) and `snapshotYears` (the years whose sorted NOMINAL values come back in `snapshots`). The result also carries `leverageZeroedCount` (RV2). With the defaults the output is the old one (`paths` kept, the existing tests untouched).
- **ONE run = the three scenarios on the same seed** (`MONTE_CARLO_SEED`, a fresh `createSeededRandom` per scenario, RV7). `H = max(50, horizon)` up to 60: the run keeps years 1…H, so the Tappe table reaches 50 years without a second run and a shorter horizon costs nothing.
- **Reading, not running** (the declared exception to The Stale-Run Rule): the threshold and the horizon within `H` are readings of the same snapshots and update at once (`summarizeProjection` is a binary search). A horizon past the years the run kept makes the run stale (`haveProjectionInputsChanged`, P11) and the figures stay the last run's, capped at `H`. Auto-run once, «Esegui» afterwards.
- **Today's euros** (RV3): the percentile of the real value is the nominal percentile over `(1 + π)^t` with the scenario's own π; the median's nominal figure sits beside it in small print in Tappe. Percentiles are floor(n × p) over ALL paths, zeroed ones included (RV4); a probability is the share of paths at or above the threshold in today's euros (RV5).
- **The starting capital** is the page's capital (`assumptions.capital.total`; since K1, 2026-10-04, the PORTFOLIO plus the chosen share of the cash to invest, doc/guide/fire.md § K1 — before it, `K`, the seven classes net of the closed pension funds; crypto, real estate, the cash not invested declared outside by `describeCapitalBreakdown` under the field), with the same «Totale / Liquido» shortcuts as the Monte Carlo. The yearly saving is seeded with the Cashflow's `annualSavings` (the Calcolatore's, the SAME `['annualCashflowData', ownerId]` query) and indexed to the scenario's inflation (RP7); «per quanti anni» defaults to the horizon, 0 means only today's capital. Locked pension funds enter as capital inflows at their unlock year at today's value.
- **The threshold (T6, § 13)** is the Calcolatore's FIRE number YEAR BY YEAR in today's euros (RN1–RN3), ONE series for the three scenarios (DN1): `resolveProjectionFireSeries(baseline, 60)` in `whatIfService.ts` = the requirement of today (`runBaselineProjection(...).fireNumberToday`, the Calcolatore's `resolveFireMetrics`) then the Base walk's `baseFireNumber` of each year (the dashed «Target FIRE» of the Calcolatore's Ventaglio) over `(1+π_b)^t`. The walk runs on the SAVED plan (`useWhatIfBaseline`, the same query keys as the What If and «Dopo il FIRE»; the draft of «Il mio piano» included) for `years` exactly (`runBaselineProjection(baseline, { years })` → `calculateFIREProjection(…, maxYears = years, …, minYears = years)`); the tab always asks for 60, so the series is a reading of the plan that does not change with the horizon typed here (a reading, RN5: no run, no «stantio» footer). A typed figure is a straight line («soglia», `ProjectionThreshold` = `{ kind: 'fire', series } | { kind: 'fixed', value } | null`); typing in the field turns the seed «Numero FIRE» off (`thresholdMode`), pressing it empties the field. Each Tappe row, the verdict and the Scenari read the threshold of THEIR year (`ProjectionFigures.threshold`); the Ventaglio draws it as a dashed series (`MonteCarloFanChart.referenceSeries`). Without a plan that runs (no expense, SWR 0) there is no series, no seed and the field starts empty (RN4). «Sotto il capitale di partenza» compares with `K`, not `K` plus what is saved.
- **Tone**: a projection is not a fact (The Risk-vs-Fact Rule), so the headline is neutral — except the probability of the DEFAULT threshold (the FIRE number series), which reads through `resolveSuccessTone` like the Monte Carlo's success rate. A typed threshold has no tone.
- **Leverage** (RV2): a year whose return wipes the capital (`1 + r ≤ 0`) leaves the path at that year's saving and it restarts from the savings; the share of such paths is said in the verdict («Con leva 1,5× il 3% delle simulazioni azzera il capitale almeno una volta.») only when there is leverage and at least one.
- **Shared pieces**: `WeightsFields` (the Allocazione block of the Parametri tile, used by both tabs; the Monte Carlo keeps its `mc-weight-*` ids), and `MonteCarloFanChart` (optional props: `zeroLine` default true, `referenceLine` — a neutral dashed line, never the destructive token —, `referenceSeries` — the same line as a series on the percentiles' years, T6 — and `markedCalendarYear`). The Distribuzione tile is gone (DN2, T6): its sentence is already in the Ventaglio's reading, and `FinalValueBars` left with it (`valueHistogram.binSortedValues` stays, `createDistribution` uses it).
- **Dated flows (F2, RF10)**: the same engine takes `flows` — the lumps land every year, a recurring flow changes the saving only while it is paid (`t ≤ savingsYears`), a FIRE-anchored flow does not exist here (the tab has no FIRE; the Parametri line says «quelli dal FIRE non valgono nella Proiezione»). The saved list is read (`useFireDatedFlows()`), the lumps of the running year join `startValue`, the list is part of the stale-run check (`datedFlowsSignature`). Values stay nominal in the run and are deflated by the scenario's π like the rest.
- **Performance**: 10.000 paths × 3 scenarios × 50 years with `collectPaths: false` measured about 0,9 s in the cloud container with and without leverage (2026-10-04, Node, no browser): under the 3 s line, so the default stays 10.000.

## CG-A — «esaurito» (2026-10-09, doc/fire-ipotesi/README.md § 21, RE4)
- A percentile ≤ 0 reads «esaurito» in the verdict, Tappe (`describeTappe`: «la mediana è esaurita», «il 10° percentile a 50 anni è esaurito»),
  Scenari and the Ventaglio's figures; the Ventaglio draws bands and median at `max(0, p)` (`MonteCarloFanChart` prop `floorAtZero`, set
  by the Proiezione only). The probabilities above the threshold are untouched.

## Composizione mobile

Composed on 2026-10-18 (mobile-light C, `doc/contributi-upstream/composizione-mobile.md` § 4, D-CU12, D-CU17): the page-wide rules — one controller per tab, the cut at the first full stop, the curve at 120px — are in `doc/guide/fire.md` § Composizione mobile; the head of the page («Il mio piano», «Ipotesi usate» as the scope line) is M3's there. The rules are DESIGN.md's (§ 5 → Small-Screen Composition). The tab is the fork's own: it follows the system, adds nothing and reorders nothing — a phone shows less, never something else; **1440 does not change** (the grid below, Tappe 12 · Ventaglio 8 | Scenari 4 · Parametri 12).

- **THE tile**: Tappe (D-CU12), first in the DOM at every width, open and never a row (id `proj-tappe`); it has no curve. The table is whole on every width.
- **The strip** (`selectProjectionStrip(summary)`, `lib/utils/projectionSummary.ts`): «Mediana tra {N} anni» → the Base median at the horizon (`currency`, the verdict's «vale … di oggi in mediana») → opens `proj-ventaglio`, `null` «esaurito» when the verdict says the portfolio is depleted; «Soglia» → the probability of the threshold (`percent`, `decimals` from `rateDecimals`: «45%», «45,2%» — the verdict's «nel 45,2% delle simulazioni») → opens `proj-scenari`, `null` «nessuna soglia» without a threshold. Tappe repeats neither. Identity in `__tests__/projectionNarrative.test.ts`.
- **The cut** (`buildProjectionVerdict`): the first sentence is the median and its range («…più di X in nove simulazioni su dieci, più di Y in una su dieci.»); the threshold and the leverage are sentences after the full stop, behind «Il perché · la soglia», «la leva» or «la soglia e la leva» (`projectionRestContent` through `fireRestLabel(…, false)`: the tab names no lock); with neither there is no cut. At 1440 the sentence is whole.
- **The rows and their aside, in DOM order** (`PROJECTION_SECTIONS` in the tab, ids `PROJECTION_SECTION_IDS` in `projectionSummary.ts`, the words `PROJECTION_CLOSED_ASIDES` and `describeProjectionParametriClosedAside(stale)` in `projectionNarrative.ts`): `proj-ventaglio` «scenario base, euro di oggi» (its fan at 120px below `desktop:`, `max-desktop:min-h-[120px]`) · `proj-scenari` «orso, base e toro» · `proj-parametri` «capitale, pesi e orizzonte», or **«modificati: premi Prova»** while the typed plan differs from the run shown (The Stale-Run Rule holds from a closed row too).
- **Without a run** (a plan that cannot run, capital zero or an allocation out of range): Parametri is the one tile on `PLAIN_GRID_CLASS` (`tablet:col-span-2`), no strip, no `PageRest`, no «Il perché» — the controller is handed `NO_SECTIONS`; the first automatic run keeps the tab's `TileGridSkeleton`.
- **The strip is the SHOWN run's** until «Prova»; the threshold and the horizon are readings (§ 11.6) and move the cells at once, a typed capital moves only the Parametri aside.
- **The memory**: `mobile-sections:fire-simulations:proiezione`, per device and browser; absent or unreadable → no `defaultOpen`: the three rows and «Il perché» start closed.
- **768 and the landscape stage** (`lib/utils/tabletComposition.ts`), with a run: Tappe on both columns, «Il resto» on a row of its own, Ventaglio beside Scenari, Parametri alone on the whole row, an open row whole; in landscape the STAGE of `doc/guide/fire.md`: verdict and Tappe on the left (Tappe has no curve to grow), the 360px index on the right with «Il resto» and the three rows.
- **The closed rows answer in the index** (landscape only; `describeProjectionClosedAnswers({ summary, startValue })`): Ventaglio «parte da {€}» (the run's starting capital, which the first screen does not print); Scenari «Orso {€}» («Orso esaurito» when depleted); Parametri null (a form is an inventory).
- **The specs**: Vitest `__tests__/projectionNarrative.test.ts` (the cut, the strip and its identity with the verdict, the asides, the answers); `e2e/fire.mobile.spec.ts` (Proiezione at 390: Tappe open, two cells, the «Soglia» cell opens Scenari with the focus on its trigger, the rows closed with an empty `inert` panel), `e2e/fire.spec.ts` at 1440 unchanged; census surface `fire-proiezione` (doc/guide/prima-schermata-misure.md).

## Per-page blind spots

- **The value is net of TER and stamp duty, gross of the tax at sale** (V8, changed by P6/D-C6, doc/fire-ipotesi § 9): the run's `annualCostRate` is `portfolioCost(run weights)`; no transaction costs. The footer of the Ventaglio says «Al netto di TER e bollo (0,36% l'anno); lordi della tassa sulla vendita» (or «Nessun costo ricorrente rilevato…» with the duty off and no TER), the Dettaglio says the same.
- **The saving grows with inflation, not with the salary**; the pension funds enter at today's value. Crypto and real estate are outside `K`.
- **Grid (T6, DN2–DN3)**: desktop Tappe 12 · Ventaglio 8 | Scenari 4 · Parametri 12; phone/tablet in column Tappe → Ventaglio → Scenari → Parametri (the DOM order, no `order-*`; since 2026-10-18 the last three are closed rows below `desktop:`, § Composizione mobile). The Tappe table is whole on every width (at most six rows) and, with the FIRE number, each row says its own figure under the percentage («di 826.000 €»).
- **The threshold is the plan's, not the Parametri's** (DN4): the FIRE number does not follow the capital, the saving and the weights typed in the Parametri tile (the field's hint says so). And the Proiezione keeps saving after the FIRE (V2) where the Calcolatore stops, so the probability of a year is not the Calcolatore's share of FIRE paths. A typed figure is a line, with no tone.
- **A new threshold does not re-run** and does not make the footer say «stantio»; a new capital, saving, weight or market assumption does.
- **The Tappe scenario selector is a view**: it changes the rows only, never the verdict (always Base).
- **Bear and Bull share the Base shocks** (same seed), so the three medians are ordered by construction; that is the point of a comparison, not a coincidence.
- **The Recharts «width(-1)» log** of the other FIRE charts appears here once on mount too: harmless.
