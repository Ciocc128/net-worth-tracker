# Draft release notes

> Accumulates until the next tag (WORKFLOW.md § Where things are recorded). Grouped by area; one entry per surface, rewritten to its final state.

## ✨ New Features

- Patrimonio › Strumenti: an ETF has a new switch, «Coperto dal cambio in EUR». Allocazione › Esposizione › Valute then counts that instrument as euro, and Impostazioni › Simulazioni › Copertura del cambio says, under a switch that is off, «Nel tuo portafoglio il Trend è coperto per il 60%» (when most of the class is held hedged) with an «Imposta coperto» button; nothing is changed until you press it and save.

- Allocazione › Bilanciamento: with an accumulation plan running, the Piano (Ribilancia, Versa, Preleva) now says what the plan buys this month, that it does not count it, and — when it proposes an instrument the plan replaced inside a group — which one the plan buys instead («compra MFEH al posto di DBMFE»), with a link to Accumulo. The optimizer's modal shows the class targets read-only, with the leverage they imply and a link to Impostazioni. Names: FIRE's goals are now «Traguardi» (tab, tiles, verdicts, Panoramica, the Impostazioni switch) and the optimizer's objectives «Criteri del modello».

- Allocazione › Accumulo: the model portfolio now leads the plan. A row of the model can be a group of equivalent instruments that buys only one of them (the optimizer evaluates it as one choice and the plan inherits it); with a running plan and no model, «Usa i pesi del piano» saves the plan's weights as the model; the last step of the plan editor can update the model together with the plan; and when the plan no longer says what the model says, the month's tile tells how many instruments differ and «Rivedi dal modello» opens the revision with the model's weights.

- Simulazioni: the market assumptions are now those of someone who invests in euro and thinks in real terms. Impostazioni › Simulazioni › Ipotesi di mercato has one real CAGR, one volatility and one uncertainty per class, and one expected inflation (no more Bear · Base · Bull selector); Obbligazioni and Liquidità follow today's ECB rates and Trend and Carry are a premium over the liquidity. Your saved figures are kept only where you had changed them, converted to real terms; Bear and Bull typed by hand are no longer used. In Calcolatore, Coast FIRE, What If and Obiettivi, Bear and Bull are now the portfolio's own 15th and 85th percentile at 30 years, with the uncertainty of the estimates; in Monte Carlo and Proiezione they are a stress where every class is at its worst or best together.

- FIRE: when the capital goes below zero the pages say «esaurito nel {anno}» instead of a negative figure — the Calcolatore's, Coast's and What If's charts stop at zero with a sign on the year, the verdict names the expense that does it («Il capitale si esaurisce nel 2032 con Acquisto Casa»), and Proiezione reads «esaurito» for a percentile at or under zero. When the FIRE does not arrive within 50 years, the Sensibilità cells and the Scenari rows tell the share of the FIRE number you will have at the target age ("20%"), «Dopo il FIRE» starts from the target age, and an Obiettivo's effect says how that share changes.

- Allocazione › Accumulo: a plan now starts from the model portfolio (the step «Dove deve arrivare il portafoglio» opens with «Parti da» — the model, today's weights or a new calculation — and a table with today's share, the target, what to buy in euro and shares, and the monthly part). A sale in the plan can be partial («Vendi quote»), and «Con vendite mirate» seeds its sales into it. A running plan can be revised from the first untouched month («Rivedi il piano»: new monthly saving, reserve, months left, weights, sales), a month's line can be recorded straight from the tile with «Registra» (the trade is prefilled and the line closes on its own), the tile proposes a recalibration only when the figures move («Applica» or «Lascia così») and a «Piani conclusi» tile lists past plans with what they invested. Step 1 suggests your average monthly saving from the Cashflow.

- FIRE › Coast FIRE: the Traguardo tile shows three stages of the same walk — today, at the target age, and at steady state (after the last pension) — each with what is needed, what you will have and what is missing, so the figure that used to sit in the Dettaglio is on the first screen. The verdict now quotes the same capital at the target age as the chart, even with an inheritance before it. The Afflussi tile lists the dated flows the Coast number counts (lumps and flows after the target age, entrates and uscite, «Afflussi e uscite» when one is an outflow) and disappears when there is nothing to list; its one line moves to the Traguardo's footer.

- FIRE › Proiezione: the «numero FIRE» it compares the portfolio with is now the very figure of the Calcolatore, recomputed every year (flows over time, pensions, tax and the locked fund move it), drawn as a dashed line that follows the plan; each row of Tappe says the figure it was compared with. A figure typed in the field stays a straight line, and the new «Numero FIRE» button takes the field back to the plan's number. Tappe now comes right after the verdict, and the Distribuzione tile is gone (its sentence was already in the Ventaglio).

- FIRE: the «Ipotesi usate» line above every tab is now a capital line and four chips — Rendimento, Costi, Spesa, Flussi — each opening a short explanation and a link to where it is changed (the Obiettivi say their flows count only in the effect on the FIRE). The Base di calcolo of the Calcolatore is a ledger: expenses ÷ SWR, then tax on withdrawals, state pensions, locked pension fund and dated flows, each row adding or taking off, adding up to the FIRE number to the euro.

- FIRE has a «Il mio piano» block at the head of the page: age, target age, yearly expense, SWR, emergency fund, flows over time, pensions, the pension lock and the IRPEF brackets are typed once, saved with one «Salva il piano» and previewed in all six tabs before saving (and taken back by «Annulla modifiche»). It opens by itself while the plan is unwritten, and an empty tab points at the missing field. It replaces the Calcolatore's Parametri and Coast FIRE's Ipotesi; the age is no longer in Impostazioni.

- The AI comment of the periodic emails now reads the private vault (fork only, for the vault's owner): the month's macro page compiled from TheBull, and for a quarter, a semester or a year the pages of the months in its window, plus the digest of the owner's investing principles. Retrieval is by date, never a search; a semester or a year receives each month cut by code (the most recent facts per area, the most recent theses, the index table), never a summary written by a model. The prompt gains six rules: the author's theses are opinions, the macro explains the market and never personal income or spending, no portfolio figure comes from the macro page and no macro figure is presented as the portfolio's, the principles guide the judgement and not the numbers, an observation that rests on a principle names it, and when the data contradict a principle the comment says so. A month without a page, or a vault that does not answer, leaves the block out and the prompt promises nothing.

- With the 50/30/20 roles on (Impostazioni › Spese), the periodic emails split the spending by role instead of by type: the «Spese per categoria» footer reads, for example, «Necessità 650 € (65,0%) · Desideri 350 € (35,0%)», and the AI comment receives the same split. Spending with no role, or with no type, is «Da classificare».

- Allocazione › Composizione ideale: a third mode, «Con vendite mirate», between Ideale (sells everything overweight) and Raggiungibile col PAC (sells nothing). Set a tax ceiling in euro and tick «Non vendere» on the instruments you want to keep: the optimizer sells first what costs least in tax for each point of target gained, and stays under the ceiling. The table shows the estimated tax on every row in sale and a total — for example «Vendi 8.000 €, paghi circa 300 € di tasse (tetto 300 €).». A high enough ceiling gives exactly Ideale's weights; a ceiling of 0 sells only positions at a loss. An instrument whose fiscal cost is unknown is never sold. If your limits in Impostazioni force a sale, the least tax that sale costs becomes the ceiling, and the total line says so.
- Every dashboard page now opens on the figures it showed last time, at once, and rereads them behind: the app keeps its last successful reads on the device (instruments, snapshots, movements, categories, settings, pension contributions, the trade ledger, cost centres, goals, the Hall of Fame and the Panoramica summary — nothing of the assistant, the benchmarks or the demo account), so a reload or a return after an hour no longer starts from a skeleton. While the fresh read is in flight the page header says so in the line under the title — «Aggiornato alle 18:42, sto rileggendo…», «Aggiornato ieri alle 18:42…» — and the sentence goes the moment the fresh figures land, without anything moving. What is kept expires after a day and is forgotten at sign-out; a save you make still shows its result the usual way, with no sentence, because nothing on screen is old then.

- Added an optional reading of Analisi › Flusso by 50/30/20 role (Necessità · Desideri · Risparmi): switch it on in Impostazioni › Spese, give each spending category a role (a subcategory can override it), and the Flusso opens on «Per ruolo» with «Per tipo» one tap away. What has no role yet reads «Da classificare», a period that spent more than it earned shows the gap as «Coperto dal patrimonio», and when some spending is itself a saving the reading says how much was set aside and how much was left over. (Contributed by Ciocc128.)

- Added, in Cashflow › Divisione, the income left «In comune» paying the shared spending first: a refund on a shared bill or a salary nobody attributed comes off the pool before the shares, the «In comune» tile shows it under its figure («Entrate in comune −300 €», «Da dividere 2110 €») and the verdict says it in the same sentence («le spese in comune sono 2410 €, meno 300 € di entrate in comune: 2110 € da dividere, 1266 € a Giuseppe (60%)…»); when the shared income covers the shared spending whole the surplus is stated («avanzano 100 €») rather than handed out. The shares now come from every income attributed to a person whatever its category, so the tab no longer depends on the categories marked as labor income and says «entrate» where it said «stipendio» («Registra le entrate di Marcella in Tracciamento e intestagliele»); the monthly email's «Spese in comune» section carries the same figures.

- Added to FIRE › Calcolatore an «Età obiettivo» tile that answers «what does it take to stop at that age?»: the yearly saving the base scenario needs, the saving that lets 9 simulations in 10 get there, and the most the plan's expenses could be with today's saving. The target age is the one of Coast FIRE and can now also be written in the Calcolatore's Parametri. Under the SWR field a «SWR personale» line proposes the withdrawal rate that lasts the horizon in 9 simulations out of 10 on your target portfolio (for example «SWR personale 4,3%: 9 simulazioni su 10 reggono 30 anni di prelievi…»), with a «Usa 4,3%» button that only fills the field until you save.

- FIRE › Calcolatore › Parametri has a «Fondo di emergenza (€)» field: the euros of the cash outside your portfolio (an account excluded from Allocazione, the excess over the Liquidità target) that you keep as an emergency fund; everything beyond it is counted by the FIRE tabs as invested on your target weights, and the field says how many months of your plan's spending the fund covers. A fund larger than that cash enters nothing and the field warns you. Whoever had saved a percentage before sees it converted into euro and is asked to save to fix it. Calcolatore, Coast FIRE, What If, Monte Carlo and Proiezione now start from the portfolio instead of from the whole net worth, and every tab says it in one line, for example «capitale 430.000 € (portafoglio 400.000 € + 30.000 € di liquidità oltre il fondo; fuori: fondo di emergenza 30.000 €, Immobili 250.000 €, Crypto 10.000 €)». With no fund set (the default) the cash outside the portfolio is not in the simulations: the year FIRE can move away for who keeps a large excluded account, and the line says where that money is.

- FIRE › Calcolatore › Parametri has a «Flussi nel tempo» section: money that enters or leaves the plan from a year, an age or the FIRE year, for a number of years or forever — a mortgage that ends (linked to the one in Patrimonio, so the end date follows your instalments), a child, an inheritance, a part-time job after FIRE, a rent. The FIRE number is today's plus the present value of those flows, the Calcolatore's projection and Coast FIRE read them, and the Base di calcolo says how far they move the year, for example «4 · spostano il FIRE dal 2034 al 2029». Monte Carlo (including Spesa sostenibile), Proiezione and the Ventaglio read them too, the same in every simulated path; What If does not yet.

- FIRE › What If has a «Quando» field on the event: leave it empty for today (nothing changes), or type a later year — a job loss in 2029, a purchase in 2031, a cashflow change from 2030 — and the plan keeps its course until then. The «prima» side now includes the dated flows of the Calcolatore, so both tabs agree, and the verdict names the year.

- FIRE › Obiettivi: a goal has an «Alla scadenza lo spendo» switch (on by default for the «Acquisto Casa» and «Auto» templates). A goal that is spent counts in the FIRE plan as an outflow in the year of its deadline, net of what you already hold outside the portfolio, and every FIRE tab reads it (Calcolatore › Parametri › «Flussi nel tempo» lists it, read-only). The goal's Traiettoria says what it does to the FIRE year, counted or not, for example «Con questa spesa il FIRE è nel 2035 invece che nel 2034 (scenario Base).», with a «Conta nel FIRE» button when it is not counted yet.

- FIRE › Monte Carlo is now «Dopo il FIRE» and answers «after the FIRE, does the capital hold, and how much can I withdraw?»: it starts, by default, at the FIRE year of the Calcolatore (on the saved plan, Base scenario) with the capital the Base has that year, or from today with the «Oggi» switch in Parametri; the verdict says it («Smettendo nel 2031 (a 45 anni) con 812.000 € di oggi, nel 63% delle 10.000 simulazioni il capitale regge fino a 90 anni…»), pensions, pension-fund unlocks and dated flows are read from that year, and the horizon runs by default to 90 years of age. Every figure is in today's euros (the nominal median stays as a column of the Dettaglio), Spesa sostenibile is the first tile, and the Distribuzione tile is gone: the mean year of depletion is in Probabilità and the percentiles every five years in the Dettaglio.

- Allocazione › Accumulo › Rivedi il piano: a running plan can now be regrouped as well. You can group two instruments or split a group, **replace the instrument that buys** (the old one stays in the group with its shares, the new one takes the same weight: «Sostituisci»), add an instrument you do not hold yet from the list «Aggiungi uno strumento» (one created and left behind is still there), change the source accounts, and take off or postpone a sale you saved but have not made yet. The rates already made stay as they were, and the Calendario names the instrument that bought them.

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

- Allocazione › Accumulo: in a group of equivalent instruments the target step now shows the figures (today's share, target, amount and shares to buy) on the instrument that buys, at its own price — it used to show them on the first of the list; the Calendario names the asset classes instead of printing their internal keys and its row buttons are 44px on touch.

- Panoramica: when the month's income so far is a sliver of its expenses the verdict says «Finora le spese superano le entrate di 1482 €» instead of a savings rate of −1926%.

- Allocazione: a sub-category target inside a class set to 0% is no longer reported as «target senza asset ribilanciabili».

- Patrimonio: an instrument created empty and never bought wears «Mai comprato» instead of «Azzerato».

- FIRE › Calcolatore and Coast FIRE: with dated flows (an inheritance, a rent after FIRE) the Bear scenario could reach FIRE before the Base, because a lower return made the future inflows worth more today. The flows are now valued at the Base return in all three scenarios: Bear, Base and Bull differ only in how the capital grows, and a worse market can no longer bring FIRE forward.

- FIRE › Calcolatore › Età obiettivo: «Risparmio per 9 percorsi su 10» showed «— · Ventaglio non disponibile» whenever the plan reached FIRE well before the target age; it now runs the simulations up to the target age (within 40 years) and shows the figure.

- FIRE › What If: the «Numero Coast oggi» left out the tax on withdrawals, so it read lower than the same number in the Coast FIRE tab (for example 200.000 € against 240.000 €), and the verdict quoted the lower one; both tabs now show the same figure.

- FIRE › Obiettivi › Come funziona: «Il rendimento atteso» still described fixed per-class rates (7% for equities, 4% without an allocation); it now explains the real rule — the Base scenario of the market assumptions, net of costs, on the goal's allocation or the target portfolio — and a new entry explains the probability and the 10°–90° band.

- Fixed the FIRE simulations diluting the portfolio weights with accounts excluded from Allocazione: a 70/30 target is now simulated as 70/30, not as 63/27 plus 10% of cash that the plan does not hold.

- Fixed the periodic emails reading the portfolio differently from the app: the verdict, the tiles and the AI comment's data now use the pages' own rules. «Mercato» is Storico's Driver, measured instrument by instrument, with savings, sale taxes, mortgage, pension contributions and other changes as rows that add up to the growth, instead of «growth minus savings», which counted a pension contribution as market. The allocation is measured like Allocazione: on the allocated portfolio, with excluded assets out, leverage and the effective targets, and the 5/25 rule. For example «Azioni 70% contro un target del 70%» where the email compared 50% of the whole net worth with the same target. «Andamento per classe» separates what the market did from what you bought, so a month of PAC instalments no longer reads as growth. The comment also receives the period's purchases and sales per instrument, and the period's return (TWR) on Rendimenti's base.
- Fixed the light/dark switch revealing only the sidebar: the circle now spreads from the button over the whole page, header and tiles included, instead of the page simply fading.

- Fixed the phone's bottom bar disappearing and popping back at every change of page: it now stays in place while the page changes beneath it, and only the highlight slides to the new section.

- Closing any dialog or bottom sheet now hands the keyboard focus back to the control that opened it, on every page: 27 of the 40 dialogs still dropped it on the page body, so a keyboard user landed at the top of the page after every «Salva» or Escape.

- Captions, reading lines and inactive labels meet the AA contrast floor (4,5:1) on the page, on tiles and on pills in all twelve colour themes: six were below it — the default light theme on pills, Retro Arcade dark at 2:1, Midnight Bloom light at 2,9:1 — and a test now holds the floor.

- Every switch (Impostazioni, FIRE, the dialogs) has a 44×44 touch target without changing its look.

- Fixed account balances and bond coupons showing long tails of digits in their edit forms (for example «4033,050000000001» as a balance, «6,500000000000001» as a coupon per unit). A balance is now stored to the cent at every movement and a coupon as the decimal it is, without changing any figure shown at two decimals.

- Fixed the asset and cash-account windows on Patrimonio closing without their animation or leaving the keyboard focus nowhere: both now slide away as every other window does and hand the focus back to the button or row that opened them. Editing an account from its detail returns to that account's row in Liquidità once the form closes.

- Fixed «Aggiorna» on Rendimenti with a custom period selected leaving the page on «Servono almeno due snapshot mensili…»: the refresh now drops the custom range and returns to the year to date.

- Fixed the Panoramica showing old figures for up to five minutes after a change it reads from: renaming a category, changing its type, moving or reassigning its movements, recording, editing or deleting a dividend that is not credited to an account, saving goals (from FIRE › Obiettivi or from the assistant), adding or removing a pension contribution, and changing the goals or pension-return settings on their own. Each of these now refreshes the Panoramica at once.

- Fixed a cost centre deleted or renamed from Cashflow › Centri di Costo keeping its old chip on the movements in Tracciamento for a few minutes: the rows it rewrites are now reread at once.

- Fixed Cashflow › Tracciamento going back to its loading skeleton every time a movement was saved or deleted: the page was rereading the dividends for the Dividendi tab (even when that tab had never been opened) and hiding the month meanwhile. A save now updates the counters and the list in place, and the dividends are reread only where they are shown.

- Fixed a category edited in Impostazioni › Spese (name, type or role) keeping its old values in Analisi for up to five minutes: the change now arrives at once.

- Fixed the smallest categories of the Analisi › Flusso chart being painted black — invisible on a dark theme — when a branch held more than seven categories: a shade now never goes darker than 55% of its colour.

## 🔧 Improvements

- Wording: the FIRE ventaglio speaks of the «allocazione target», the optimizer's dialog is «Ricalcola il portafoglio modello», Impostazioni names the model portfolio where it said «composizione ideale», and Allocazione's header on the Accumulo tab says where its weights come from.

- FIRE and Impostazioni › Simulazioni: the three market scenarios are now called **Bear · Base · Bull** everywhere (labels, charts, tables and verdicts — «il bear lo sposta al 2036»), the way an Italian investor says them, instead of Orso and Toro.

- FIRE › Monte Carlo and Proiezione › Parametri: of «Usa i target» and «Importa il portafoglio di oggi», the source in use is now shown pressed (filled), like the other toggles of the app, instead of greyed out as if it were unavailable.

- The weekly TheBull page in the private vault is now compiled by DeepSeek V4.1 Flash with the reasoning off instead of GLM 5.3 Flash, which failed about one week in five (slow or broken answers); the periodic emails stay on GLM, now only on its full-precision hosts. Every item still has to quote the newsletter word for word. (Fork only.)

- Added to FIRE › Monte Carlo a «Spesa sostenibile» tile that answers «how much can I withdraw?»: the largest yearly withdrawal, in today's euros, that lasts the whole horizon in 80, 90 and 95% of the simulations, for the bear, base and bull scenarios, replayed on the same simulated paths as the run on screen (same capital, weights, leverage, costs, pensions and tax on withdrawals). The verdict gains a sentence — for example «Per restare al 90% potresti prelevare fino a 43.300 € l'anno di oggi (3608 € al mese), il 4,3% del capitale.», or, when the withdrawal you typed is too high, «Per tornare al 90% il prelievo dovrebbe scendere a 43.300 €…» — and with a leverage that wipes the capital out in more than one simulation in ten, it says that no withdrawal reaches 90%. The figures follow the last run: edit the plan and press «Esegui» to update them.

- Improved FIRE › Calcolatore, Coast FIRE, What If, Monte Carlo, Proiezione and Obiettivi: every return now comes net of what your instruments cost every year, TER and stamp duty, instead of gross. Each class takes the TER of the instruments you hold in it (a trend-following fund and the carry class are already net in their default returns, so they pay only the duty) and the stamp duty switched on in Impostazioni › Allocazione › Costi; the cost is taken off the capital every year after the return, so a 60/40 with ETFs at 0,20% and 0,10% and the duty on goes from a Base of 8,26% to 7,87%. The «Ipotesi usate» line says it («costi 0,36% (TER 0,16%, bollo 0,20%)», or that the duty is off, or that no TER is entered), and Impostazioni › Simulazioni shows the net return of the target portfolio. Proiezione, which used to say «Valori lordi», is now net of TER and stamp duty and gross only of the tax at sale.

- Added FIRE › Proiezione, a sixth tab that answers «how much could my portfolio be worth in N years, and with what probability?». From your capital and, if you want, a yearly saving (read from the Cashflow, growing with inflation, for as many years as you choose), it simulates the three scenarios on the same hypotheses as the other tabs and shows the median and the 10th and 90th percentile in today's euros, the odds of beating a threshold (by default your FIRE number), a milestone table at 10, 20, 30, 40 and 50 years and the distribution at the horizon you pick. For example «Tra 30 anni il portafoglio vale 714.000 € di oggi in mediana; più di 213.000 € in nove simulazioni su dieci. Supera il numero FIRE di 800.000 € nel 45% delle simulazioni.». Changing the threshold or the horizon updates the figures at once; values are net of TER and stamp duty and gross of the tax at sale.

- Improved FIRE › Calcolatore, Coast FIRE, What If and Monte Carlo: they now share the same capital, the same yearly expenses and the same savings rule. The capital is what the plan simulates (your seven simulated classes, with real estate and crypto declared outside and closed pension funds excluded) instead of a different net worth per tab; the yearly expense is a single «Spesa del piano» you set in Calcolatore › Parametri (falling back to last year's Cashflow, or to the Coast FIRE expense you had typed before, which migrates on the next save); and savings grow with inflation, so a 12.000 € saving today is about 12.400 € next year, where the Calcolatore used to keep it flat. The «Ipotesi usate» line now states the capital and the expense, and what is left out.

- Improved FIRE › Calcolatore, Coast FIRE, What If and Monte Carlo: they now start from the same hypotheses. The three scenarios are the compound return of your Allocazione target portfolio, computed on the per-class assumptions of Impostazioni › Simulazioni (a 60/40 with the defaults gives 6,0% · 8,3% · 11,1%), instead of three rates typed by hand; the real return is now `(1 + growth) ÷ (1 + inflation) − 1` rather than a subtraction; the Ventaglio simulates the target portfolio, not the one held; and a new «Ipotesi usate» line above each verdict says which portfolio, returns and inflation the numbers run on. Impostazioni › Simulazioni shows what the typed numbers return on your target portfolio. The rates saved in Parametri › Scenari are no longer used.

- Improved FIRE › Obiettivi: a goal's expected return now comes from the same hypotheses as the other FIRE tabs, instead of its own table (equities 7%, bonds 2,5%…). With a recommended allocation it is that allocation's compound return on the Base scenario of Impostazioni › Simulazioni (80% azioni + 20% obbligazioni gives 9,2%; crypto and real estate are left out and the rest rescaled, and the footer says so); without one it is the Base return of your target portfolio. The tab shows the same «Ipotesi usate» line as the others, and the Assistant quotes the same return.

- Improved the FIRE › Monte Carlo and the Ventaglio: they now simulate seven classes — Azioni, Obbligazioni, Oro, Materie prime, Liquidità, Trend and Carry — so the cash on your accounts earns what cash earns instead of the portfolio's mix, and the legs of a composite ETF count in their own class. Crypto and real estate stay out of the simulated capital, and the Parametri tile says how much (for example «Fuori dalla simulazione: Immobili 250000 €»). The return you type is now a CAGR, the compound growth of the median year, and the volatility drives a lognormal draw: with 7% and 18% the median compound growth is 7%, where the old average read 5,5%. The assumptions live in the new Impostazioni › Simulazioni tab, with a source and a period for every class (long-run history in dollars, 3,04% inflation in all three scenarios) and the arithmetic mean shown beside each pair; Monte Carlo and Ventaglio read the same numbers. Scenarios saved before are carried over keeping their mean and variance, and real estate is dropped. Saving new assumptions marks the last Monte Carlo run as out of date.

- Improved the FIRE › Monte Carlo and the Ventaglio: the seven classes now move together instead of independently. A new «Correlazioni» tile in Impostazioni › Simulazioni lists the 21 pairs, class by class (the ones you hold first), from −1,00 to 1,00 in steps of 0,05, starting from long-run values with a source (for example equities and bonds barely related, equities and trend following slightly opposed). If the pairs you type cannot all be true at once, Save adapts them to the nearest consistent set, tells you how many pairs moved and keeps «scritto 0,90 → usato 0,50» next to each until the next Save. The Parametri tile says whether the correlations are the defaults or yours, the explanation no longer says the classes are independent, and one matrix serves the three scenarios and the Ventaglio. Correlations are yearly and do not rise in a crisis, as the explanation states.

- Improved the FIRE › Monte Carlo and the Ventaglio: they now simulate a leveraged portfolio. Weights above 100% are leverage: the tile starts from your Allocazione targets (targets of 90% and 60% start a 1,5× portfolio) or, with «Importa il portafoglio di oggi», from what you hold, leveraged ETFs included, and the Parametri tile prints «Totale 150% · leva 1,5×»; below 100% or above 300% the run stays blocked. The borrowed part costs what cash earned that year plus a spread you set in Impostazioni › Simulazioni (2,0% to start, what a leveraged ETF costs over the ECB rate).
